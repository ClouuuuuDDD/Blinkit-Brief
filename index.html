Strategic Analysis

Strengths

  • Zomato ecosystem: capital, user base, tech infrastructure.
  • First-mover advantage in quick commerce operations.
  • Early profitability in mature markets.

Weaknesses

  • High operational costs (dark stores, delivery fleet).
  • Thin margins on core grocery items.
  • Geographic concentration in metros.

Opportunities

  • Advertising platform growth (high-margin revenue).
  • Private label expansion (higher margins).
  • Zomato One membership integration.

Threats

  • Zepto's aggressive funding and expansion.
  • Swiggy Instamart's ecosystem advantage.
  • Regulatory scrutiny on dark store zoning.
